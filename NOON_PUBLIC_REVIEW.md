# IKTEFAA 3.0 Public Review Brief

## Strategic proposition

IKTEFAA 3.0 is designed as a connected operating layer across commerce, food security, physical stores, inventory, fulfilment, logistics, fleet operations, merchant networks, payment orchestration, governance, and measurable social impact.

The central proposition is integration. A consumer order, a store inventory position, a supplier shipment, a delivery route, a merchant identity, a settlement condition, and an impact outcome can be represented in one traceable operating model.

## Why this matters to a scaled commerce operator

The model can support a strategic operator that already has customer reach, merchant relationships, logistics capability, payment partnerships, and data infrastructure. The public MVP makes the operating logic visible through:

- A unified executive dashboard.
- Separate operating workspaces for commerce, stores, supply chain, logistics, merchants, payments, impact, analytics, and governance.
- Searchable coverage of all 103 recorded IKTEFAA 3.0 features.
- End-to-end demonstration scenarios that connect commercial activity with fulfilment, settlement, evidence, and impact.
- A clear boundary between the technical platform layer and the physical operating assets required for deployment.

## Asset boundary

The technical platform layer includes the digital architecture, application logic, feature register, identity and evidence model, contract and rule orchestration, analytics, payment-orchestration concepts, and governance interfaces.

The operating project layer includes physical stores, warehouses, distribution centres, inventory, fleet, suppliers, personnel, customer operations, working capital, and field execution.

These layers are shown together because they create the complete operating proposition, but they remain separately identifiable for technical review, commercial structuring, implementation planning, and transaction due diligence.

## Demonstrated public flows

1. A smart basket moves from demand and recommendation through store inventory, fulfilment, fleet assignment, delivery proof, and impact recording.
2. A merchant moves from digital identity and trust review to acceptance, operational status, and payment loyalty.
3. A food shipment moves from strategic supplier registration through origin proof, quality control, cold-chain visibility, and inventory receipt.
4. A transaction is classified against programmable terms and governance rules, with a human review path for a decision requiring approval.
5. A donation or waqf contribution is directed to an impact objective and represented through a traceable distribution record.
6. A food-security signal creates a forecast, identifies an operational risk, and recommends a replenishment action.

## Technical review route

Start with `index.html`, open the executive dashboard, and inspect each operating domain. Use the feature registry to search by feature number or name. Use the scenario runner to generate an event trail in the dashboard. Read `README.md` for the public scope, production boundary, and ownership notice.

## Production path

The public MVP is intentionally self-contained and uses synthetic data. A production programme would require a governed service architecture, authentication, data protection, observability, resilience, security testing, payment-provider integration, regulatory approvals, independent Shari'ah review where applicable, operating-site validation, and formal acceptance testing.

## Important qualification

This public review brief is an architecture and operating demonstration. It is not a valuation report, a regulatory filing, an offer of financial services, a payment licence, a banking service, an insurance product, a Shari'ah certification, or a transfer of intellectual-property rights.
