# IKTEFAA 3.0 Marketplace and Commerce Public Review Brief

## Strategic proposition

IKTEFAA 3.0 is designed as a platform-neutral operating and control layer for marketplace operators, electronic-commerce companies, retailers, omnichannel commerce groups, distributors, and other organisations that coordinate commerce across multiple parties and channels.

The architecture is intended to operate above or alongside existing commerce environments. It can connect customer and merchant channels with identity, evidence and provenance, contracts and rights, inventory, fulfilment, logistics, payment orchestration, settlement, governance, analytics, and measurable impact through controlled integration points.

IKTEFAA is not positioned as a replacement marketplace or as a competing consumer channel. The intended model is to complement existing customer interfaces, order-management systems, fulfilment capabilities, delivery networks, payment relationships, and enterprise systems, subject to future technical, commercial, legal, regulatory, security, and Shari'ah validation.

Illustrative review audiences may include global and regional operators such as Amazon, Alibaba, Noon, and comparable businesses. These names are used only to describe the category of potential strategic reviewers. They do not imply affiliation, endorsement, partnership, customer status, or existing integration.

## What the public MVP makes visible

- A unified executive dashboard for reviewing the connected operating model.
- Separate workspaces for commerce, stores, supply chain, logistics, merchants, payments, impact, analytics, and governance.
- Searchable coverage of all 103 recorded IKTEFAA 3.0 features.
- End-to-end demonstration scenarios that connect commercial activity with fulfilment, settlement, evidence, and impact.
- A layered architecture that separates the digital platform from physical operating assets and existing enterprise systems.
- An integration position that can be evaluated against marketplace, retail, wholesale, and omnichannel environments.

## Platform and integration boundary

The technical platform layer includes the digital architecture, application logic, feature register, identity and evidence model, contract and rule orchestration, analytics, payment-orchestration concepts, settlement logic, and governance interfaces.

The integration boundary is intended to connect with existing channels and systems through governed application programming interfaces, events, data contracts, and policy controls. A future implementation may connect to commerce channels, merchant systems, catalogues, inventory, orders, warehouses, fulfilment, carriers, fleets, payment providers, settlement services, enterprise resource planning, data platforms, and approved external services.

The operating project layer includes physical stores, warehouses, distribution centres, inventory, fleet, suppliers, personnel, customer operations, working capital, and field execution where a deployment requires them. These assets are not automatically included in the public software demonstration.

These layers are shown together because they create the complete operating proposition, but they remain separately identifiable for technical review, commercial structuring, implementation planning, partnership, licensing, or transaction due diligence.

## Demonstrated public flows

1. A smart basket moves from demand and recommendation through store inventory, fulfilment, fleet assignment, delivery proof, and impact recording.
2. A merchant moves from digital identity and trust review to acceptance, operational status, and payment loyalty.
3. A food shipment moves from strategic supplier registration through origin proof, quality control, cold-chain visibility, and inventory receipt.
4. A transaction is classified against programmable terms and governance rules, with a human review path for a decision requiring approval.
5. A donation or waqf contribution is directed to an impact objective and represented through a traceable distribution record.
6. A food-security signal creates a forecast, identifies an operational risk, and recommends a replenishment action.

## Technical review route

Start with `index.html`, open the executive dashboard, and inspect each operating domain. Use the feature registry to search by feature number or name. Use the scenario runner to generate an event trail in the dashboard. Read `README.md` for the platform-neutral integration position, public scope, production boundary, and ownership notice.

## Production path

The public MVP is intentionally self-contained and uses synthetic data. It does not contain a live integration with Amazon, Alibaba, Noon, or any other marketplace or commerce operator. A production programme would require a governed service architecture, authentication, data protection, observability, resilience, security testing, payment-provider integration, regulatory approvals, independent Shari'ah review where applicable, operating validation, and formal acceptance testing.

## Important qualification

This public review brief is an architecture and operating demonstration. It is not a valuation report, a regulatory filing, an offer of financial services, a payment licence, a banking service, an insurance product, a Shari'ah certification, proof of live commercial performance, or a transfer of intellectual-property rights.
